The rules

Five questions every day.

Shople takes a verified food product from a UK supermarket, adds a random UK location as part of the question, and asks you to guess the displayed price. Where a retailer publishes a loyalty price, the question can be either the standard price or the loyalty-card price.

The five questions are generated deterministically from the date, so everyone gets the same daily set. Scoring rewards accuracy. An exact guess gets 1,000 points; the score falls as your guess moves further from the actual price.

Data model

September 2026 only.

The current build is deliberately a fixed September 2026 dataset. The database is kept in a standalone data.js file so you can manually replace prices each year without changing the game.

Retailer online prices can differ from in-store prices, and promotions or stock can change. Location is therefore a gameplay label unless store-specific pricing is available from the source.
