The rules

Five questions. One daily score.

1
See the productWe give you a UK retailer, product and pack size.
2
Check the price conditionShople tells you whether you are guessing the standard price or a named loyalty-card price.
3
Lock in your guessAn exact guess scores 5,000. The further away you are, the lower the score.
4
Finish all fiveYour total is out of 25,000 and your streak is updated when the round is complete.
5
Share itYour result can be turned into a shareable link that anyone can open.
Loyalty pricing

We make the card requirement explicit.

A question can be a standard price or a loyalty-card price. The exact card is displayed directly on the question so there is no ambiguity.

Examples: Tesco Clubcard, Sainsbury's Nectar, Co-op Member Card and Lidl Plus.

A standard-price question can still come from a product that has a loyalty offer; the game simply tells you to guess the non-member price. The discounted amount is not revealed until after you submit.

Data

September 2026 only.

The game uses a fixed September 2026 price snapshot. Each record stores its retailer, product, pack size, standard price, loyalty price where available, named loyalty card, verification date and source URL.

Shople does not assign a fictional branch or regional price. Every question is based on the retailer/product price captured in the September 2026 dataset.

Annual refresh

Update the data, not the game.

Next September, replace the product data file with the new year's prices, update the snapshot date and commit the changes to GitHub. Vercel will redeploy automatically.

data.js is intentionally separate from the game logic to make this easy.
